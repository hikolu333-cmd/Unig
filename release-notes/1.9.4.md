# UniGeek 1.9.4
*2026-11-17*

The LVGL migration lands everywhere: every game, the watchdog pack, the Chameleon and file-manager packs and the attack screens now render through the LVGL shell on USE_LVGL_SHELL boards — no TFT-only corners left in the menus. Beacon Flood gains a real network-count picker, the chip temperature shows on the home page and during attacks, and the mascot picker got its long-overdue crash fix.

## New Features

> **Beacon Flood: network count picker**
> Choose 10/20/50/100/150/200 or Endless. The count caps the pool of *distinct* fake networks (not the run length — the pool keeps cycling until BACK). Beyond the 70 built-in names the list grows with generated variants (`FreeWiFi 071`), and every network keeps a **stable BSSID** derived from a per-attack random base, so entries persist in the victim's scan list instead of churning out on every cycle. The send loop runs at ~250 SSIDs/s: a 150-network pool cycles in ~600 ms.

> **Chip temperature, home + attacks**
> The home page shows the ESP32 internal sensor reading (5 s refresh, colour-graded: grey → orange ≥65 °C → red ≥80 °C). Attack screens carry a corner badge — Beacon Flood (both modes) and Deauth via a panel sprite, BLE Spam / Evil Twin / Karma Captive via an LVGL label that hides when the attack stops.

> **Distinct-network counter on Beacon Flood**
> The live panel now shows `Nets: X / N` — how many unique fake networks are already on air, next to the send rate and the detector-threshold indicator.

## Fixes

> **Mascot picker crashed on open**
> `InputSelectAction::popup` reads `sublabel`/`hasRssi` from every option row, but ~20 call sites (Settings → Mascot among them) declared their option arrays on the stack without initialization — the renderer followed garbage pointers. All call sites now zero-initialize; the LVGL modal opens cleanly.

> **Mascot selection now actually applies**
> The shell hardcoded the boykisser photo and showed other mascots as a labelled circle. Hacker/Cat/Robot now draw their real art onto the home page through a sprite→canvas bridge (scaled into an 80×102 box), blink and sway with the same timers as the photo, and pick up theme colour + rank accents. Changing the mascot in Settings applies on return to the home page.

## Internals

> **SpriteCanvas bridge (ui/lvgl/SpriteCanvas.h)**
> Reusable sprite→lv_canvas bridge with byte-swapped RGB565, an explicit-parent `ensure()` and reuse across frames — the mascot and the game screens share it.

> **Captive portal folders (sdcard)**
> `school4/` (login/password form) and `freewifi/` (two-step SMS flow, asks phone → code → password) portal templates ship in the repo's `sdcard/unigeek/web/portals/`; drop them on a card via USB MSC and point Evil Twin / Karma at the folder.
